Footwear · cowboy boots and fashion

Cowboy boots are our specialty.

Our design and development team makes cowboy boots and on-trend fashion footwear for brands in the US, UK and Europe, using leather from our own tannery.

In the factory

Embroidery, stitching and lasting, all in house.

Our automated embroidery machines create the decorations on cowboy boot shafts. On the stitching floor, our team assembles the panels, then the uppers are lasted and the soles attached.
